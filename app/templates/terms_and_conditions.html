1. Program Name

Product Connections Event Manager SMS Alerts

2. Program Description

Product Connections Event Manager SMS Alerts is an internal workforce notification service that sends text messages to designated supervisors when employees submit calloff (absence) reports through the Product Connections Event Manager application. Messages contain the employee's name, calloff date, reason category, and the number of scheduled events affected. This service is used exclusively for internal workforce management and is not a marketing program.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on employee calloff activity. Supervisors can expect to receive:

  • Typically 1 to 5 messages per week, depending on team size and calloff volume.
  • One message per calloff submission (one employee calling off = one SMS).
  • Occasional additional messages if a supervisor manually requests an SMS resend.

Message volume will not exceed normal business communication needs. No recurring scheduled messages are sent.

4. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Standard messaging rates from your wireless carrier apply to all SMS messages sent and received. Contact your carrier for details about your text messaging plan.

5. Opt-Out Instructions

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time:

  • Text STOP to the number from which you received the message. You will receive a single confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent.
  • Alternatively, contact your system administrator to have your phone number removed from the SMS notification configuration.

Opting out of SMS does not affect in-app push notifications or any other Application functionality. Calloff submissions will continue to appear in the supervisor dashboard regardless of SMS opt-out status.

6. Help and Support

For assistance with SMS notifications:

  • Text HELP to the number from which you received the message for instructions.
  • Contact your system administrator or Club Supervisor for support with the Product Connections Event Manager application.
  • Email: admin@pcevents.com

7. Supported Carriers

SMS messages are delivered via Twilio's messaging platform, which supports all major U.S. wireless carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and others. Carrier support is subject to Twilio's carrier network. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
